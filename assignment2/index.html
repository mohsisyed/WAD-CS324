Tips

  • Stick to 2–3 main colors in your outfit. Neutral shades like black, white, beige, and navy are easy to combine.
  • Wear clothes that fit your body comfortably. The right fit makes even a simple outfit look stylish and polished.
  • Complete your look with a watch, belt, sunglasses, or a bag. A few well-chosen accessories can make a big difference.
